About Our Model

A More Organized Approach to Mineral Commerce

Mineral commerce depends on reliable sourcing, appropriate material assessment, processing, logistics, and trusted commercial relationships.

Lexy Mineral Technologies brings these activities into a disciplined operating model.

By combining mineral purchasing, processing access, sampling and testing, aggregation, and trading, the company improves how mineral materials are evaluated, prepared, and brought to market.

Founder Background & Market Fit

Built on Practical Mineral Industry Experience

Lexy Mineral Technologies is informed by hands-on experience in mineral purchasing, processing operations, material assessment, concentration, logistics, and recordkeeping.

Beginning in 2023, the founder gained practical grassroots field exposure across key Nigerian mineral regions including Plateau State, Zamfara State, Kaduna State, Maiduguri, and Bauchi State, personally handling 11 strategic minerals ranging from Gold and Tin Ore to Tantalite, Columbite, Monazite, Zircon, Rutile, Iron Ore, Coal, Silicon, and Copper-bearing materials.

This deep field experience forms the operational backbone on which Lexy is being scaled into a structured, revenue-generating mineral enterprise.

Operating Model

Source

04

05

Trade

Supply refined mineral products to established industrial buyers and partners.
