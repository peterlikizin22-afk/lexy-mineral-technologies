Building Better Mineral Processing Infrastructure

Lexy Mineral Technologies is developing accessible mineral-processing, testing and verification infrastructure for legitimate small-scale miners in Nigeria.

Explore Our Solution

The Problem

Many small-scale miners have limited access to reliable processing equipment, mineral testing, accurate grade verification and legitimate buyer connections. These gaps can reduce the value miners receive from their production and make transparent transactions more difficult.

Our Solution

Lexy Mineral Technologies is developing a shared processing model that can allow legitimate miners to access processing and verification services without each miner having to own expensive infrastructure.

Processing

Shared access to mineral-processing infrastructure designed for small-scale mining operations.

Testing & Verification

Mineral testing and documented results to support clearer information about material quality and grade.

Records

Better documentation of processing activities, material information and verified results.

Buyer Connections

A future pathway for connecting verified mineral outputs with legitimate buyers.

Founder Experience

The idea is grounded in hands-on experience within Nigeria's small-scale mineral-processing and trading sector. The founder has experience working as a mineral buyer, processing-mill operator, supervisor, records personnel and ore transporter across several locations in Nigeria.

How It Works

01 — Miner

Legitimate small-scale miners bring mineral material for processing.

02 — Processing

Material is processed through shared infrastructure.

03 — Verification

Material can be tested and the results documented.

04 — Market

Verified outputs can be connected with legitimate buyers.

Our Vision

Our long-term vision is to build a network of accessible mineral-processing and verification hubs that can improve efficiency, transparency and market access for legitimate small-scale miners across Nigeria.
