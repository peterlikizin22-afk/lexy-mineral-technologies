About Our Model

A More Organized Approach to Mineral Commerce

Mineral commerce depends on reliable sourcing, appropriate material assessment, processing, logistics, and trusted commercial relationships.

Lexy Mineral Technologies is developing a business that brings these activities into a more organized operating model.

By combining mineral purchasing, processing access, sampling and testing, aggregation, and trading, the company aims to improve how mineral materials are evaluated, prepared, and brought to market.

Our long-term vision includes digital tools for maintaining mineral records, documenting quality information, and improving operational visibility as the business develops.

Founder Background

Built on Practical Mineral Industry Experience

Lexy Mineral Technologies is informed by hands-on experience in mineral purchasing, processing operations, material assessment, concentration, logistics, and operational recordkeeping.

Beginning in 2023, the founder gained practical exposure to mineral-related activities across several mineral-producing regions of Nigeria, including Plateau State, Zamfara State, Kaduna State, Maiduguri and surrounding areas, and Bauchi State. This experience included working with existing processing mills, assessing materials before purchase, participating in mineral concentration activities, and supervising the movement of mineral materials.

These experiences helped shape an understanding of the practical challenges involved in sourcing, evaluating, processing, and bringing mineral materials to market. Lexy Mineral Technologies is being developed to build on that foundation.
