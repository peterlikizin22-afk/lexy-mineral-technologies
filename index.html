About Lexy

Built from firsthand mineral-field experience.

Lexy Mineral Technologies is an early-stage Nigerian mining-technology venture being developed around practical experience in mineral buying, processing, supervision, records, transportation and material assessment.

01

Field Experience

Approximately three years of practical exposure across Plateau, Zamfara, Kaduna, Bauchi and Borno/Maiduguri.

02

Operational Insight

Experience includes buying, processing-mill operations, manual washing and concentration, supervision, records and ore transportation.

03

Core Opportunity

Make processing, verification, documentation and market connections more organized and accessible for legitimate mineral operators.

Mission

To build practical infrastructure and technology that improves mineral processing, verification, traceability and market connectivity across Nigeria's mineral value chain.

Vision

To become a trusted mineral infrastructure and technology platform connecting responsible mineral suppliers, processors, buyers and technical partners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions partners and customers may have.

Which minerals does Lexy focus on?

Initial experience and interest include iron ore, tantalite, columbite, monazite, zircon, rutile, tin, gemstones, mica and silicon/silicon-bearing materials. Acceptance will depend on legality, testing, market requirements and available capacity.

Can small-scale miners access Lexy?

Yes. The model is specifically intended to make processing, sampling, testing and market support more accessible to small-scale and independent miners.

Will Lexy serve medium and large-scale operators?

The developing model is intended to support a wider range of operators as technical capacity, infrastructure and compliance systems grow.

Does Lexy currently operate its own processing facility?

No. At this development stage, Lexy is working toward its own shared processing and verification infrastructure and may use suitable partner mills for early operations.

How does Lexy's verification approach work?

The intended workflow is based on proper material identification, representative sampling, testing or analysis where appropriate, documentation and clear communication of results.

How can investors or technical partners collaborate?

Potential collaboration areas include equipment, processing, testing, geology, logistics, digital systems, responsible supply chains, funding, formalization and business development.
