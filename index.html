About Lexy

Practical mining experience with a scalable vision.

Lexy Mineral Technologies is an emerging Nigerian mineral-sector venture focused on building practical infrastructure around mineral processing, verification, aggregation and buyer connections.

The idea is straightforward: many miners can access mineral-bearing materials but may not have reliable access to efficient processing, useful grade information, structured records or dependable connections to legitimate downstream buyers.

Lexy is being developed to help address these gaps through a structured, transparent and scalable operating model.

Our Vision

To develop a trusted network of shared mineral-processing and verification infrastructure that connects miners, processors, investors and legitimate buyers while improving transparency across Nigeria's mineral value chain.
